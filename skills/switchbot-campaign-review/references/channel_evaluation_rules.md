# Channel Evaluation Rules

## Shared principles

- Evaluate each channel against its intended role and available evidence.
- Separate observed delivery from attributable sales.
- Use `Insufficient Data` when required denominators or outcomes are unavailable.
- Do not infer causality from simultaneous traffic and sales changes.

## Performance media

Apply to Google Ads, Meta Ads, Yahoo Ads, Amazon Ads, and direct-response campaigns. Review Spend, Impressions, Clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, Conversions, CVR, CPA, Revenue, ROAS, scale, and marginal efficiency. Investigate higher spend without proportional conversion or revenue growth.

## YouTube and brand media

Review Reach, Frequency, CPM, CPV, VTR, completion rate, effective views, view-through or assisted conversions, brand-search movement, and creative performance. Do not reject brand media solely for low direct ROAS.

## PR

Review original coverage, syndicated coverage, media tier, content quality, product coverage, persistence, search value, and potential reach. Keep original and syndicated counts separate.

## KOL and influencer

Review content count, views, engagements, engagement rate, cost per view, cost per content, product fit, affiliate sales, content quality, and long-tail views. Missing affiliate revenue does not mean zero sales.

## CRM: EDM, LINE, App Push

Review sends, delivery rate, opens, open rate, clicks, unique clickers when available, CTR, conversions, revenue, unsubscribes, send time, frequency, topic, and audience overlap.

## SNS and Organic

Review reach, engagement, sharing, content spread, follower or search growth, referral traffic, and tracked conversion. Avoid treating untracked exposure as worthless.

## Decision vocabulary

Use exactly one: `Expand`, `Slightly Increase`, `Maintain`, `Optimize and Continue`, `Slightly Reduce`, `Significantly Reduce`, `Pause`, `Continue Testing`, or `Insufficient Data`.

Always include evidence, confidence, and risk. Budget recommendations must account for scale, marginal return, brand role, minimum effective budget, stability, and measurement quality.
