# Campaign Review Analysis Framework

## Required sequence

1. Define the decision: overall performance, drivers, resource allocation, and next action.
2. Inventory sources and comparability before calculating.
3. Present the executive conclusion before detail.
4. Separate scale, efficiency, contribution, and confidence.
5. Diagnose anomalies with alternative explanations.
6. Convert findings into budget direction and accountable actions.

## Executive questions

- Did the campaign meet the available target?
- Is performance better, stable, or worse than the nearest comparable period?
- Which changes are real efficiency changes rather than budget, duration, mix, or scope effects?
- Which channels and products contributed most, and which dragged performance?
- What is the highest-impact data or execution problem?
- What should management expand, maintain, optimize, reduce, pause, or continue testing?

## Evidence labels

- `[Fact]`: directly supported by reviewed data.
- `[Insight]`: synthesis of multiple facts.
- `[Hypothesis]`: plausible but not confirmed explanation.
- `[Recommendation]`: proposed action.
- `[Data Gap]`: missing evidence that blocks or weakens a conclusion.
- `[Risk]`: factor that could materially change the interpretation.

## Root-cause structure

For each material anomaly, state what changed, the supporting data, plausible causes, alternative explanations, business impact, how to validate, and the next action.

## Management output order

1. Executive Summary
2. Core Findings
3. Overall Performance
4. Channel Evaluation
5. YoY and Trend Analysis
6. Highlights
7. Problems and Root Causes
8. Budget Recommendations
9. Action Plan
10. Data Limitations
