# Trend Analysis Rules

## Separate the drivers

For every comparison, distinguish scale change, efficiency change, budget change, channel mix change, product mix change, campaign-duration change, measurement-scope change, and external cost change. Do not call higher Spend or Impressions an improvement by itself.

## Trend threshold

- One comparison: describe YoY or period-over-period change only.
- Two periods: do not claim a sustained trend.
- Three or more comparable periods: classify sustained improvement, sustained deterioration, stable, high volatility, one-time anomaly, structural shift, diminishing return, or emerging opportunity.

## Confidence

- `High`: at least three comparable periods, consistent direction, and no material quality issue.
- `Medium`: sufficient periods but some volatility or minor scope differences.
- `Low`: few periods, missing metrics, or material comparability limits.

## Direction heuristic

- Positive metrics: Revenue, Conversions, ROAS, CTR, CVR, Reach.
- Negative metrics: CPA, CPC, CPM, cost per view.
- Context-only metrics: Spend, frequency, impressions, sends.

Report the numeric path and confidence; do not hide contradictory periods behind a single label.
