# Brand Copy Checklist

Before approving a Brand Story or Brand Introduction block, verify:

- Is this a company-level Brand Promise, a product philosophy, or a product feature?
- Is the wording supported by a current official source or accepted project decision?
- Does the block add brand meaning instead of repeating the product hero message?
- Are ecosystem references real and current?
- Are cross-sell products current, relevant and source-backed?
- Does the copy avoid unsupported superlatives, No.1 claims, awards, scale claims and vague AI-style slogans?
- Does the visual language feel like the same brand without copying the product hero composition?
- On mobile, does the brand hierarchy remain visible?

If the block fails the first three checks, revise before production.
