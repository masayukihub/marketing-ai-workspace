# Generic Copy ↔ Render Gap Examples

These examples illustrate QA logic only. They are not approved product claims.

## Scale

Copy: “compact body”

Weak render: product floating in an empty premium living room.

Stronger proof: product shown against a verified furniture/door/measurement reference that makes scale readable.

## Mechanism

Copy: “roller cleaning mechanism”

Weak render: ordinary lifestyle floor-cleaning scene.

Stronger proof: approved mechanism asset, contact detail or verified diagram makes the roller/action visible.

## Performance number

Copy: a specific suction/performance number.

Weak render: dramatic particles flying toward the product.

Required evidence: approved numerical claim plus a visual treatment that does not pretend a decorative scene is test proof.

## Automation

Copy: “automatic maintenance”.

Weak render: product parked beside a station.

Stronger proof: approved sequence or interface clearly shows the actual maintenance step(s).

Use these only as QA patterns. Real assets must be evaluated against current Product Truth, source evidence and approved claims.
