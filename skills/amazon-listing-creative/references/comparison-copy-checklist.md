# Comparison Copy Checklist

Before approving a comparison block, verify:

- Is the mode explicitly `Internal Competitor Matrix` or `Publishable Series Comparison`?
- If publishable, are all compared products/variants allowed by current channel policy and formal source data?
- Does the first screen help a shopper choose (`Recommended For`, `Home / Usage Fit`, `Key Differentiator`) before raw specs?
- Does every factual cell have a current source?
- Are price, ratings, awards, No.1 and performance claims omitted unless explicitly approved?
- Does the current product avoid artificial advantage from omitted fields or biased wording?
- Does mobile use a readable stacking/scroll strategy?
- Are internal cross-brand competitor findings excluded from the publishable asset?

If publishability is unknown, keep the comparison in internal review only.
