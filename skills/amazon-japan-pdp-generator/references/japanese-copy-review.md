# Japanese Copy Review

## Required process

For every product-image and A+ content-unit Headline:

```text
Raw selling point
→ user benefit
→ natural Japanese consumer expression
→ short Headline
→ Supporting Copy
→ source-backed Proof
→ three options
→ score and select Final
```

Do not translate Chinese syntax. Do not use vague AI-style expressions such as `革新的`, `新次元`, `圧倒的`, `これまでにない` or excessive abstract nouns unless an approved Claim and real consumer benefit require them.

## Three options

- Option A: recommended benefit-first expression.
- Option B: more functional and mechanism-led.
- Option C: more lifestyle and situation-led.

Score each 1–5 on:

1. Japanese naturalness;
2. Amazon five-second readability;
3. product and Claim accuracy;
4. mobile length.

Record all options, scores, selected option and reason in the PDP JSON and `reports/japanese_copy_review.md`. A compatibility-generated option is an `Auto Draft — Needs Japanese Copy Review`, not a human-approved final.

## Writing rules

- Prefer 5–15 Japanese characters for product-image Headline.
- Use one clear user benefit; do not stack features with punctuation.
- Supporting Copy is one short sentence. Move conditions and specs to Level 3.
- Keep A+ copy compact: Headline + supporting copy + proof. Do not place a 3–4 line paragraph as the visual center.
- Break Japanese at meaning boundaries, not every fixed number of characters. Avoid leaving particles at line starts or one/two characters alone on a line.
- Keep conditions when they materially limit compatibility, setup, measurement or performance.
- Never use internal status language in consumer copy.

## Final questions

- Does this sound like a Japanese EC designer wrote it?
- Is the benefit understood before the mechanism?
- Can it be read on a 390 px screen?
- Is every factual implication supported by the cited Claim?
- Is this image saying something different from the previous image?
