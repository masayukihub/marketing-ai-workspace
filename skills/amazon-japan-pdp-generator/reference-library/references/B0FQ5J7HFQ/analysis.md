## Visual Grammar

| Dimension | Observation |
|---|---|
| visual hierarchy | Sparse black headlines and calm product photography precede detailed workflow cards. |
| information density | Low to medium in gallery scenarios; high inside workflow and plan modules. |
| image text ratio | About 70:30 in scenario units and 45:55 in software-output explanations. |
| whitespace | Generous warm-neutral whitespace is a defining rhythm device. |
| full bleed usage | Used selectively for video and lifestyle closure rather than every module. |
| card usage | Cards model inputs, outputs and workflow stages; they carry semantic sequence rather than decoration. |
| product scale | Dominant early and in physical-detail modules; deliberately small in outcome-led scenes. |
| typography hierarchy | Large restrained headline, short subcopy, small interface labels; calm and editorial. |
| dark light rhythm | Mostly warm light with a dark video hero as the primary contrast break. |
| scene technical rhythm | Category explanation alternates with realistic work scenes and output demonstrations. |
