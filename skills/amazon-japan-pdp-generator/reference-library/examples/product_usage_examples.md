## Common rule across all three

- Reference selection happens after KNOW and before PLAN.
- The matcher proposes Top 3; it does not approve claims, product truth, layout or final production.
- A selected reference can supply story architecture, technology-explanation discipline or visual rhythm. It cannot supply competitor copy, images, trade dress or a complete layout.
- Production still uses only the 19 existing templates, official SwitchBot Product Layers and programmatic Graphic Layers.
