# Layout Primitive Mapping

> The 19 existing templates remain the only production layouts. This mapping reclassifies each template as a bounded layout primitive and assigns semantic Visual Roles; it does not add templates.

Visual Roles: Hero / Technology / Scenario / Feature / Proof / Comparison / Detail / Closure

| Template ID | Family | Layout Primitive | Primary Role | Secondary Roles | Best When | Reference Learning | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-MAIN-OFFICIAL | product_image | Centered object on white | Hero | Detail | IMAGE-01 must establish exact product identity without marketing copy. | All six references separate a neutral official main image from the persuasion sequence. | Official Product Layer only; No text, badge, scene or inferred accessory |
| P-HERO-SPLIT | product_image | Asymmetric text-product split | Hero | Feature / Closure | One value proposition must be understood before details. | Eufy and Plaud show that a calm hero can establish fit or workflow value without a feature collage. | One hero value; Product at least 42 percent; Maximum three information levels |
| P-FEATURE-CENTER | product_image | Centered product with top message and limited callouts | Feature | Technology / Detail | A visible part or interaction is the single visual center. | Soundcore product cutaways and Eufy mechanism units work when the object stays central and callouts are limited. | One mechanism; Maximum two callouts; No radial feature inventory |
| P-FEATURE-SPLIT | product_image | Balanced object-copy split | Feature | Proof / Detail | One feature needs a short benefit and one optional source-backed note. | Levoit and Eufy repeatedly pair one visible mechanism with one shopper consequence. | One feature and one benefit; No multi-topic collage; Text remains readable at 390px |
| P-LIFESTYLE-FULL | product_image | Full-bleed scene with controlled product and overlay zones | Scenario | Hero / Closure | A believable Japanese-life moment answers a concrete purchase question. | Plaud, Levoit and Eufy use scenes as fit evidence and rhythm resets, not decoration. | Official product remains accurate; One user moment; Scene cannot carry unsupported proof |
| P-TECHNICAL-PROOF | product_image | Object or diagram plus stacked proof zone | Proof | Technology / Detail | An invisible mechanism or performance claim needs a source-linked explanation. | Soundcore and Eufy are strongest when one mechanism resolves one performance question; ECOVACS shows the density limit. | Maximum three proof items; Every claim has source and condition; All diagram text is programmatic |
| P-ECOSYSTEM | product_image | Centered hub with verified relationship map | Technology | Feature / Detail | Compatibility and control relationships are a purchase decision. | Plaud workflow maps and Eufy control closure are useful only when relationships stay explicit and limited. | Verified relationships only; No invented UI or logos; Mobile becomes a vertical list |
| P-COMPARISON | product_image | Selection headline plus bounded comparison matrix | Comparison | Proof / Closure | A small number of source-backed criteria will change the shopper's choice. | All six references use comparison, but Soundcore and Plaud show that full tables are often too dense for Gallery. | Prioritize selection criteria; Unknown values stay internal; Do not force a full desktop table into mobile |
| P-PURCHASE-CONFIDENCE | product_image | Product anchor plus stacked purchase checks | Closure | Detail / Proof | Included items, installation, support, privacy or compatibility can block purchase. | Eufy closes with support, privacy and ecosystem; Levoit closes with household fit. | Maximum three checks; Separate included and required items; No vague assurance |
| A-HERO | aplus | Wide opening split with one visual center | Hero | Scenario / Feature | A+ must reframe the category and value without replaying Gallery. | Plaud and Levoit use the A+ opening to reset the experience around workflow or audience, not a feature list. | Use once; One category/value statement; Mobile text precedes image |
| A-50-50-FEATURE | aplus | Equal narrative and visual columns | Feature | Technology / Proof / Scenario | One mechanism-to-benefit explanation needs more room than Gallery. | Eufy keeps mechanism explanations readable by giving one question a complete horizontal unit. | One primary feature; One optional note; Stack text then image on mobile |
| A-THREE-FEATURE-GRID | aplus | Two or three equal semantic cards | Feature | Detail / Proof | Two or three parallel items answer the same consumer question. | Plaud input modes and Eufy three-reason explanations work because cards are parallel; ECOVACS inventories are not. | Exactly two or three parallel cards; Equal image scale; Never a miscellaneous feature collage |
| A-LIFESTYLE | aplus | Wide scene with narrative overlay | Scenario | Hero / Closure | The page needs to prove fit, routine or relief in a believable context. | Levoit uses a dark bedroom as a rhythm change; Plaud uses work scenes to prove workflow fit. | One moment; Product remains accurate; Crop-safe face and product zones |
| A-TECHNICAL | aplus | Large technical visual plus proof column | Technology | Proof / Detail | The shopper needs mechanism, condition and consequence together. | Soundcore demonstrates credibility; ECOVACS demonstrates why density must be capped. | Maximum three proof cards; Claim conditions remain adjacent; No invented diagram text |
| A-INSTALLATION | aplus | Ordered two- or three-step sequence | Detail | Proof / Closure | Setup effort or accessory sequence is a purchase barrier. | Eufy's fit-first story shows installation information is confidence content, not a technical afterthought. | Verified steps only; Official installation assets; No inferred accessory or omitted prerequisite |
| A-ECOSYSTEM | aplus | System relationship map with editable labels | Technology | Feature / Detail | The product's value depends on verified devices, apps or platforms working together. | Plaud's workflow chapters and Eufy's control closure show that relationship maps must follow the user's task. | Verified edges only; No invented UI; Spokes convert to vertical relationships on mobile |
| A-COMPARISON | aplus | Wide source-backed selection matrix | Comparison | Proof / Closure | The user must choose among current variants or understand a method difference. | Comparison works as closure across all references but becomes exhausting when every row is treated equally. | Priority rows first; Uniform scale and current sources; Selection aid, not superiority theater |
| A-FAQ | aplus | Stacked objection-response rows | Closure | Detail / Proof | Compatibility, requirements or conditions remain after the story. | Soundcore's FAQ converts a dense technical story into final purchase clarity. | Maximum three priority objections; Question and answer stay adjacent; No tiny disclaimer-only answers |
| A-BRAND | aplus | Brand statement plus official product or scene | Closure | Hero / Proof | An approved brand promise materially reduces purchase risk. | Competitor Brand Stories often overuse awards; SwitchBot should use brand only when it supports the product decision. | One approved brand message; Official logo asset; No unsupported leadership or award claim |
