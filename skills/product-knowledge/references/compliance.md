- Product: AI MindClip; KATA Friends
- Market: Japan
- Compliance Item: Recording, transcription, AI-generated content, privacy, permissions, retention, and deletion

- Product: All products
- Market: Japan
- Compliance Item: Battery life, speed, noise, accuracy, coverage, suction, energy savings, awards, and No.1 claims
